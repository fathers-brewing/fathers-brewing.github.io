Founded with purpose

Better for you. Absolutely delicious.

Fathers was founded to create a beer that's not only better for you, but tastes absolutely delicious. The founders, whose beverage of choice has been beer their whole lives (past the legal drinking age of 21, of course), noticed that their go-to brews came with things that disappointed them: lead, Roundup, PFAS "forever chemicals" and microplastics.

They wanted to enjoy a beer with peace of mind that what they were drinking was clean, while keeping the true essence of a good brew.

As for the name, the role of a father is creating something valuable for the next generations. Being dads themselves, the founders aim to do the same with clean lagers. Beers that have been around for decades have served their purpose, but it's time to choose a beer that prioritizes transparency and wellbeing. And of course, clean beer simply tastes better.
